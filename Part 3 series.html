Perfect Crown (2024)

Character: Grand Prince Yi-an

XByeon Woo-seok plays Grand Prince Yi-an, a royal prince who has a prestigious title but limited freedom because of his family's expectations. His life changes when he enters a contract marriage with Seong Hui-ju, a wealthy businesswoman who wants royal status. As they spend more time together, their relationship develops into something deeper despite royal traditions, political conflicts, and personal struggles.

Lovely Runner (2024)

Character: Ryu Sun-jae

He plays Ryu Sun-jae, a former competitive swimmer who becomes a famous singer and the lead vocalist of the fictional band Eclipse. Despite his popularity, he has personal struggles and secretly loves Im Sol. Their story becomes complicated when Sol travels back in time to change their future. This drama combines romance, comedy, music, and fantasy.

Strong Girl Nam Soon (2024)

Character: Ryu Shi-oh

He portrays Ryu Shi-oh, a wealthy and mysterious CEO who secretly leads a criminal organization. He is intelligent, ambitious, and manipulative, but he also has a complicated past. His character becomes interested in Kang Nam-soon, creating a complicated connection between them. This role shows Byeon Woo-seok's darker side as an antagonist.

Moonshine (2024)

Character: Lee Pyo

He plays Lee Pyo, a rebellious Crown Prince during the Joseon Dynasty. Despite his royal responsibilities, he enjoys living freely and secretly drinking alcohol despite strict government prohibitions. Beneath his playful personality, he has emotional struggles and wants to make his own decisions. His character brings romance, comedy, and royal drama to the story.

Record of Youth (2024)

Character: Won Hae-hyo

He plays Won Hae-hyo, a wealthy and talented model who dreams of becoming a successful actor. Although he comes from a privileged family, he wants people to recognize his abilities instead of relying on his family's influence. His friendship with Sa Hye-jun becomes complicated as they pursue similar careers and face competition in the entertainment industry.

Flower crew:Joseon Marriage Agency (2024)

Character: Do Joon

He portrays Do Joon, a charming and intelligent member of the Flower Crew matchmaking agency.
