MIDNIGHT RUNNERS (2017)

Character: Club Entertainer

The movie follows two police academy students, Ki-joon and Hee-yeol, who witness a kidnapping and decide to investigate the case themselves. Their adventure leads them into a dangerous situation as they try to rescue the victim. Byeon Woo-seok appears briefly as a club entertainer in a cameo role. Although he isn't one of the main characters, it's an interesting early appearance for fans who want to follow his filmography.

ASHFALL (2019)

Character: Bodyguard

Ashfall is a disaster-action movie about the eruption of Mount Baekdu, which threatens the Korean Peninsula. A special team is formed to prevent an even bigger catastrophe and save millions of people. Byeon Woo-seok appears in a minor role as a bodyguard. Although his screen time is limited, this is another early movie appearance in his acting career.

2OTH CENTURY GIRL (2022)

Character: Poong Woon-ho

Set in 1999, the movie follows Na Bo-ra, a high school student who promises to gather information about her best friend's crush. While doing so, she becomes close to Poong Woon-ho, played by Byeon Woo-seok. Woon-ho is a kind and thoughtful student who enjoys photography and belongs to the broadcasting club. As they spend more time together, their friendship gradually develops into a sweet first-love story.

Soulmate (2023)

Character: Ham Jin-woo

This movie tells the story of two girls, Mi-so and Ha-eun, whose friendship begins during childhood and continues as they grow older. Their relationship experiences love, jealousy, misunderstandings, and separation. Byeon Woo-seok plays Ham Jin-woo, a young man who becomes romantically involved with Ha-eun but also develops feelings connected to Mi-so. His character becomes an important part of the complicated relationship between the two best friends.
