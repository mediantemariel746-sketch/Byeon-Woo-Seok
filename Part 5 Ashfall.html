Ashfall

Ashfall (Korean: 백두산; RR: Baekdusan), also known as: Mount Paektu, is a 2019 South Korean disaster film directed by Lee Hae-jun and Kim Byung-seo, starring Lee Byung-hun, Ha Jung-woo, Ma Dong-seok, Jeon Hye-jin and Bae Suzy. The film was released in December 2019 in South Korea.

Plot

Ashfall (2019) is a South Korean disaster action film about the sudden eruption of Mount Paektu, an active volcano located between China and North Korea, which causes massive earthquakes and destruction across the Korean Peninsula. To prevent another devastating eruption, Jo In-chang, a South Korean special forces captain, is assigned to lead a dangerous mission involving a plan to use a nuclear bomb to release the pressure beneath the volcano. He teams up with Lee Joon-pyeong, a North Korean agent who has valuable knowledge about the mountain. As they travel through dangerous areas, they encounter American soldiers, Chinese gangsters, and several unexpected obstacles that threaten their mission. Meanwhile, In-chang's pregnant wife, Choi Ji-young, struggles to survive the disaster in Seoul. Despite the challenges, In-chang and Joon-pyeong work together to save millions of lives. In the end, Joon-pyeong sacrifices himself to detonate the bomb and stop the final eruption. The movie concludes with the Korean Peninsula beginning its recovery, while In-chang reunites with his family and takes responsibility for Joon-pyeong's daughter.

Production

Production on Ashfall ended on 21 July after five months of filming.

Kim Yong-hwa was in charge of production, and Dexter Studio, represented by Kim Yong-hwa, was in charge of VFX and co-produced and distributed with CJ. It is a co-directed work by Lee Hae-joon and Kim Byung-seo.

Release

The film was released 19 December 2019 in South Korea. Internationally, it was released on 20 December in the United States, 24 December in Taiwan, 1 January 2020 in Hong Kong, 2 January in Singapore and Malaysia, 8 January in Indonesia, the day after in Thailand and Australia, and 31 January in Vietnam.
